# Everyday accounts

The account tile and the savings chip share one screen, with savings, debit cards and other services. Balance, optional card/SMS services, audience and sorting stay in browser memory. Neither input edits nor toggles send requests or persist user amounts. The catalog endpoint rejects balance/amount fields; the comparison endpoint accepts `product_type=account_fee`, `item` and `audience` for compatibility.

Income uses a printed savings slab for the selected audience. A maximum-only rate is displayed as a maximum and never supplies net income. NCC NXT has audience `check`, remains available in source detail only, and is excluded from comparisons pending eligibility verification. General maintenance ladders are not silently assigned to a special-audience account.

Maintenance and half-yearly SMS fees are annualized twice. VAT is added only when documented; VAT-inclusive fees are not taxed again. The EBL debit facts do not explicitly supply VAT, so no VAT is assumed for them. Net is shown only when interest and every selected cost are known for the balance. Unknown costs never become zero. With default SMS on, BRAC has no net figure. At BDT 100,000, SMS off and card on, BRAC's example is 500 − 460 − 690 = −650; with card and SMS both off, it is +40. Published maximum fees make these illustrative, not guaranteed returns.

Only printed maintenance boundaries are used. BRAC has the full supplied ladder; other banks have partial slabs. NCC and Dutch-Bangla account-fee rows are stale and excluded under the current display override, even though NCC appears in an older six-bank example. EBL unreadable maintenance/closing/cheque/minimum facts are hidden. Missing facts and unsupported net values have no filler rows.

Standard debit products are primary, other debit types are variants, and prepaid types are excluded from comparisons but remain inspectable in detail. BRAC and EBL Classic have published base annual fees of BDT 600 each. Original names, dates, amounts, conditions and quotes are retained in source sheets. CSV changes classify twelve EBL variant/excluded rows; preview normalizes on read without reloading the shared database.

The seven fixed-default narration slots cover general savings, debit, other services and four audiences. Published fact sheets and default calculations may be drafted by the configured model under owner authorization. Numeric, slab, risk and wording checks are required (`account_readout.check_draft` checks numeric drafts before pronunciation; manual review remains required), followed by text review before paid offline synthesis. Source/text/audio hashes gate saved playback; runtime readout does not synthesize speech. Deposit narration fingerprints must be refreshed after audience metadata changes without rewriting approved text silently.

Checks cover exact yearly math, boundary gaps, maximum-rate exclusion, incomplete-net suppression, audience isolation, prepaid classification, API privacy and browser state. Browser automation covers Small/Extra large, portrait/rotation, keyboard-height resize, desktop and 200% text. Physical-device, screen-reader and listening acceptance remain outstanding.
