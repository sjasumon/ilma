## Review notes

- Facts come from the current published seed, using fresh main cards. No new bank facts or risk evidence were added.
- Meta drafted candidates from the approved published fact sheets. Candidates were checked against the seed; wording was locally revised or replaced with a fact-based template to fit the requested shape. This is a text review draft, not listening acceptance.
- Tied annual fees are grouped. Interest is annual; cash percentage ranges and minimum-taka ranges are separate. Detailed bank-specific cash combinations remain on screen.
- The first-free-extra-card counts are five for Visa Classic and Gold, six for Platinum, three for Signature, and two for World and Infinite. Titanium has one such bank, whose first two cards are free. These counts include conditional first-card waivers even where later additional cards have a price.
- The example’s universal no-interest promise and all-fees VAT wording were not copied: the seed does not support those blanket claims. VAT is mentioned once, for applicable fees.
- No dates, sources, stale-bank notices, missing-data notices or closing disclaimer are spoken.
